run_const bool AC_compact_poisson = false

This file implements iterative solvers for the Poisson equation in Cartesian,cylindrical and spherical coordinates. Includes Jacobi,Gauss-seidel and Successive over-relaxation. Most likely not efficient enough on their own but a good starting point for setting your initial problem correctly.
